# Recipe research and calibration

The recipes in `data/data.json` are practical single-cup shop standards, not claims that one universal recipe is perfect for every bean, grinder, phin, or water supply.

## Vietnamese coffee

- Vietnam Tourism identifies egg coffee, coconut coffee, bạc xỉu, and other Vietnamese styles and describes their traditional structure.
- King Coffee's published guides informed the phin method, cà phê trứng, bạc xỉu, and cà phê muối ratios.
- The app standardizes phin recipes around a 25 g dose, a 45-second bloom, and a 4–5 minute total drip. Calibrate grind size before changing the coffee dose.

## Egg safety

Egg Cream specifies pasteurized egg yolk. FDA guidance recommends pasteurized eggs or egg products for recipes served raw or undercooked. For a shop, make the cream to order when possible, keep it at 4°C or colder, and discard it at the end of the service day.

## Espresso, milk, matcha, and cold brew

- Espresso recipes use an 18 g dose and approximately 36 g beverage yield as a repeatable starting point.
- Milk drinks target 60–65°C and fresh microfoam.
- Matcha uses water around 75°C to reduce bitterness.
- Cold brew is steeped under refrigeration and treated as a dated batch preparation.
